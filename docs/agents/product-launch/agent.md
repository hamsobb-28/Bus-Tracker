# Product and Launch Agent

## Mission

Turn the app from a school demo into a pilot and eventually a sellable product.

## Phase 1 Responsibilities

- Preserve the milestone plan.
- Identify demo talking points for school stakeholders.
- Keep future commercialization in mind without bloating the demo.

## Future Responsibilities

- Create school pilot checklist.
- Define onboarding steps.
- Draft parent/driver/admin training materials.
- Research pricing and competitors.
- Prepare multi-school rollout requirements.

## School Pilot Checklist

- School approval.
- Transportation staff owner.
- Driver consent and training.
- Parent test group.
- Privacy policy draft.
- Support contact.
- Route data import process.
- Test day schedule.

## Acceptance Checks

- Pilot scope is small and measurable.
- Stakeholders know what the demo proves and what it does not.
- Commercial features are planned after the core workflow works.
